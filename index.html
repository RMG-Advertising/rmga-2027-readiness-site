    Scoring key

    1. Score each statement: Not yet = 0, Sort of = 1, Nailed it = 2.
    2. Multiply your Human Touch section total by 1.5.
    3. Add it to your other five section totals (maximum 60). Divide by 60 and multiply by 100 for your score out of 100.
    4. Human Touch Index: add your Human Touch section total (unweighted) and your four human-thread items (1.5, 3.5, 4.5, 5.3). Divide by 20 and multiply by 100.

    Tiers: 0–39 Rewind Mode · 40–64 Warming Up · 65–84 Ready to Roll · 85–100 Built for 2027.
